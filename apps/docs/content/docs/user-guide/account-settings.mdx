## Profile

### Edit Your Profile

1. Go to **Profile → Profile**
2. Click **"Edit Profile"**
3. Update your **Profile Picture**, **Display Name**, **Bio**, or **Website**
4. Click **"Save"**

### Privacy Settings

| Setting | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Public Profile** | Enables a public profile page visible to anyone |
| **Share Stats** | Shows download counts on your public profile |
| **Social Links** | Twitter, GitHub, LinkedIn, etc. |

## Account

### Change Email

1. **Profile → Profile → Email**
2. Enter your new email address
3. Click the verification link sent to the new address

Keep access to your old email until the change is confirmed.

### Change Password

1. **Profile → Security → Change Password**
2. Enter your current password
3. Enter and confirm your new password

**Password requirements:**
- Minimum 8 characters
- Mix of uppercase and lowercase letters
- At least one number
- At least one special character

### Delete Account

**Profile → Data → Delete account**, then confirm with your password.

<Callout type="error" title="This is permanent">
  All files, URLs, domains, and data are immediately and irreversibly deleted.
</Callout>

## Security

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

1. **Profile → Security → Enable 2FA**
2. Install any TOTP app (Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator, etc.)
3. Scan the QR code
4. Enter the code from the app to confirm
5. **Save your backup codes** in a safe place

To disable, go to **Profile → Security → Disable 2FA** and confirm with a current code.

### Upload Token

Your upload token is used by integrations (ShareX, Flameshot, API clients) to authenticate uploads.

- Find it at **Profile → Uploads → Upload Token**
- Click **"Regenerate"** to invalidate the old token and issue a new one

<Callout type="warn">
  Regenerating your token will break all existing integrations until they are updated.
</Callout>

### Active Sessions

View and revoke active login sessions under **Profile → Security → Sessions**.

## Notifications

Everything Emberly tells you about appears under the bell at the top of the dashboard and on **Dashboard → Notifications**, where you can filter by category, mark notifications read or clear them. Notifications are kept for 90 days.

Under **Profile → Notifications**, choose which categories are also emailed to you or posted to [Discord](/integrations/webhooks):

| Category | Examples | Email | Discord |
|----------|----------|-------|---------|
| **Security** | New sign-ins, password and 2FA changes, API keys | On | On |
| **Account** | Email changes, applications, shared files, data exports | On | Off |
| **Billing** | Payments, receipts, subscriptions, refunds | On | On |
| **Product updates** | Perks, extra storage, new features | On | Off |
| **Announcements** | Offers and newsletters | Off | Off |

The switches at the top of the Email and Discord columns turn that channel off entirely. Password resets, sign-in links and verification codes are always emailed.

## Billing

- **Profile → Billing → Upgrade Plan** — instant, pro-rated
- **Profile → Billing → Manage Subscription** — cancel, downgrade, or view invoices
- **Profile → Billing → Payment Method** — update card details
