Emberly is free to self-host. A license is only needed to remove Emberly's branding, or to run Emberly as a hosted service for other people. Licenses are sold at [licenses.embrly.ca](https://licenses.embrly.ca).

---

## Free Branding

Every instance can set:

- **Site name** and **description**, in **Admin → Settings → License & branding**. The name is used in the page title, navigation and emails.
- **Favicon**, in **Admin → Settings → Appearance**.
- **Footer**: you can hide the site footer, in **Admin → Settings → License & branding**.

Unlicensed instances show a small "Powered by Emberly" credit, even with the footer hidden, and keep the Emberly logo.

## What a License Adds

| Feature | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **White-label** | Upload your own logo, remove the "Powered by Emberly" credit, and use your name in emails and link previews. |
| **Hosted service** | Allows Cloud mode (`EMBERLY_RUN_CLOUD=true`): billing, custom domains, squads, Discovery and managed storage. Required to offer Emberly to others as a service. |

## Installing a Key

1. Buy a license at [licenses.embrly.ca](https://licenses.embrly.ca). You enter the domains your instance runs on, such as `files.example.com` or `*.example.com`.
2. Copy the key from the email or from your portal.
3. Open **Admin → Settings → License & branding**, paste the key and click **Save**. It should show your license as **Active**. Upload your logo there.

You can set the key as an environment variable instead, for example to manage it with the rest of your deployment:

```env
EMBERLY_LICENSE_KEY="emb_lic_..."
```

A key in the environment takes priority, and the admin panel then shows it as set on the server.

The key must match the host in `APP_URL`. `localhost` always works, so you can test a key locally.

## How Keys Are Checked

Keys are signed. Emberly checks the signature on your own server, so your instance keeps working if licenses.embrly.ca is unreachable.

Once a day, a licensed instance checks in with licenses.embrly.ca. It sends the key, a random instance ID, the domain and the Emberly version. The check-in:

- Picks up the renewed key when your subscription renews, so you never have to paste a new one.
- Counts how many servers use the key, which is limited by your plan.
- Learns whether the key was cancelled or revoked.

Unlicensed instances never check in.

### Offline Servers

If your server can't reach the internet, turn check-ins off:

```env
EMBERLY_LICENSE_CHECKIN="false"
```

You then need to copy a new key from your portal after each renewal.

## When a Key Stops Working

| Status | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| Expired | Licensed features switch off until a renewed key arrives. Everything else keeps working. |
| Wrong domain | The `APP_URL` host isn't one of the key's domains. Change the domains in your portal and the next check-in fixes it. |
| Revoked | Licensed features switch off. |

Your files, users and settings are never affected.

## Managing Your License

Sign in at [licenses.embrly.ca/portal](https://licenses.embrly.ca/portal) with your email to:

- Copy your key
- Change its domains
- See which servers use it
- Open Stripe billing to update your card, download invoices or cancel
